# SECOND-BRAIN-KNOWLEDGE-PAPER.md — Forensic Document Examination Research Advisor

A curated knowledge base of research papers, books, and authoritative sources underpinning this skill's methodology. These should be consulted (and their principles extracted into skill reference files) when building the actual skill.

> **Note on sourcing:** This list was compiled from general subject-matter knowledge to give the skill a strong research foundation to build from. Before relying on any specific citation (e.g., in an academic, legal, or professional deliverable), verify the exact title, year, and venue independently, as this list has not been individually re-verified against live databases at compilation time.

## Research Papers & Sources (20)

1. ASTM International (2020). ASTM E2290: Standard Guide for Examination of Handwritten Items. ASTM — Foundational standard forensic-document-examination methodology guide.
2. SWGDOC (Scientific Working Group for Forensic Document Examination) (2018). Standards for Examining Documents. SWGDOC — Foundational professional FDE methodology standards.
3. National Research Council (2009). Strengthening Forensic Science in the United States: A Path Forward. National Academies Press — Landmark critical review of forensic-science reliability, including document examination.
4. M. Kam, J. Wetstein, R. Conn (1994). Proficiency of Professional Document Examiners in Writer Identification. Journal of Forensic Sciences — Landmark empirical study on FDE examiner accuracy/error rates.
5. S. Sita, B. Found, D. Rogers (2002). Forensic Handwriting Examiners' Expertise for Signature Comparison. Journal of Forensic Sciences — Empirical research on examiner expertise and validated accuracy.
6. J. Osborn (1929). Questioned Documents (2nd ed.). Boyd Printing — Foundational classical text of forensic document examination.
7. R. Huber, A. Headrick (1999). Handwriting Identification: Facts and Fundamentals. CRC Press — Standard comprehensive FDE methodology textbook.
8. D. Ellen (2005). Scientific Examination of Documents: Methods and Techniques (3rd ed.). CRC Press — Standard comprehensive document-analysis technical reference (ink, paper, indented writing).
9. M. Kam, G. Fielding, R. Conn (1997). Writer Identification by Professional Document Examiners. Journal of Forensic Sciences — Empirical error-rate research relevant to reliability discussion.
10. B. Found, D. Rogers (1995). Contemporary Issues in Forensic Handwriting Examination: A Discussion of Key Issues in the Wake of the Starzecpyzel Decision. In Proceedings of the Australian and New Zealand Forensic Science Society — Legal-admissibility critique discussion.
11. D. Risinger, M. Denbeaux, M. Saks (1989). Exorcism of Ignorance as a Proxy for Rational Knowledge: The Lessons of Handwriting Identification 'Expertise'. University of Pennsylvania Law Review — Foundational legal-scholarship critique of FDE scientific validity.
12. American Board of Forensic Document Examiners (ABFDE) (2021). Certification Requirements and Code of Ethics. ABFDE — Professional-certification standard relevant to referral framing.
13. S. Srihari, S. Cha, H. Arora, S. Lee (2002). Individuality of Handwriting. Journal of Forensic Sciences — Empirical computational research on handwriting individuality relevant to the field's scientific basis.
14. J. Sulner (2009). The Application of a Standardized Forensic Document Examination Methodology to the Analysis of Digital Documents. Journal of Forensic Document Examination — Modern application of FDE methodology to digital/typed documents.
15. D. Kelly, B. Lindblom (eds.) (2006). Scientific Examination of Questioned Documents (2nd ed.). CRC Press — Comprehensive updated document-examination technical reference.
16. Innocence Project (2021). Forensic Science Reform: Document Examination. Innocence Project — Applied advocacy reference on wrongful-conviction risk relevant to reliability-limits education.
17. C. Bridges (1963). Practical Fingerprinting (adjacent forensic-identification methodology reference for comparative-science standards) — Comparative methodological standard from a related forensic-identification discipline.
18. R. Morris (2000). Forensic Handwriting Identification: Fundamental Concepts and Principles. Academic Press — Standard applied FDE conceptual-principles reference.
19. J. Miller (1998). Ink Dating: A Review of Current Techniques. Journal of Forensic Sciences — Applied review of ink-dating methodology relevant to document-dating education.
20. D. Foster (2000). Author Unknown: On the Trail of Anonymous. Henry Holt — Applied case-study reference illustrating (and cautioning about) authorship/handwriting-attribution methodology in real disputes.
